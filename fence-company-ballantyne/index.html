Fences & Firepits installs fences throughout Ballantyne and south Charlotte, including the 400-linear-foot 3-rail split rail fence with welded wire and two custom arched gates featured in our project gallery. Ballantyne's established HOA neighborhoods and wooded lots call for fences that look finished from the street and hold up for years.

Our Ballantyne split rail project

This 400-foot, 3-rail split rail fence uses welded wire to contain pets while keeping the open, natural look of a wooded property. Two custom arched gates complete the design.

HOA neighborhoods

Most Ballantyne neighborhoods have architectural guidelines. Black aluminum, white vinyl and natural or stained wood are common approved materials, but every HOA is different — check yours before you choose.

Recommended materials

Split rail with wire for large, wooded lots; aluminum for pools and front yards; board-on-board wood for backyard privacy.
