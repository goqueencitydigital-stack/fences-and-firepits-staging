Which privacy fence is best?

For the lowest up-front cost, a 6-foot wood privacy fence. For the least maintenance, vinyl. For no gaps as the wood ages, board-on-board. For a modern home, horizontal wood. Try each one in the Fence Design Studio to see how it looks.

Privacy for pets and kids

Solid privacy fences reduce what dogs can see, which often means less barking at passersby. Set the bottom of the fence close to grade and plan gates with secure latches.

Height and HOA limits

Six feet is the most common backyard limit; some lots allow eight. Front-yard fences are usually limited to shorter heights. We help you confirm the rules before you submit to your HOA.

Is wood right for your project?

  • Pets: Excellent for large dogs when built solid to the ground; board-on-board limits see-through gaps.
  • Pools: Possible for some layouts, but aluminum is usually preferred for pool barriers because of visibility.
  • HOAs: Widely accepted. Many architectural committees specify the good side facing out, a height limit and stain color.

How it works

From estimate to walkthrough

  1. Free on-site estimate

    Ron or a team member walks the property with you, measures the fence line, notes slopes, trees, utilities and gate locations, and talks through materials and styles that fit your goals and HOA.

  2. Written proposal

    You receive a written estimate with the material, height, style, footage, gates and scope spelled out, so you know exactly what is included before you sign.

  3. Planning and approvals

    We help you gather the drawings and specifications your HOA or architectural committee asks for. Utility locating is requested before any digging.

  4. Installation day

    Posts are set, framing and panels or pickets are installed and gates are hung, adjusted and latched. Many residential projects are completed in a short window once materials arrive; your proposal lists the expected schedule.

  5. Walkthrough

    We review the finished fence with you, check gate swing and latches, and make sure the site is cleaned up before we leave.
