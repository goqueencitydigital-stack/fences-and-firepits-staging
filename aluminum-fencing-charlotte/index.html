Overview

Fences & Firepits installs powder-coated aluminum fences in Charlotte, Huntersville, Waxhaw and nearby communities. Aluminum gives you the ornamental look of wrought iron without rust, keeps views open and needs very little maintenance, which is why it is a favorite for pools, front yards and estate lots.

Is aluminum right for your project?

Pets

Good for most dogs. Puppy-picket panels close the lower gaps for small dogs.

Pools

One of the most popular pool-fence materials: open visibility and self-closing gate hardware options.

HOAs

Frequently approved because it keeps views open; black is the most common approved color.

01

Aluminum vs. wrought iron

Aluminum looks similar to wrought iron but will not rust and is much lighter, so it is easier to install on slopes and costs less to maintain. Wrought iron is heavier and stronger but needs periodic painting.

02

Racked for sloped yards

Many aluminum panels can be racked to follow a gentle slope so the fence stays parallel to the ground. Steeper grades are stepped. We plan this on site.

03

Pools, pets and views

Aluminum is a natural choice when you want a safe, defined yard without blocking the view of your landscaping, a pond or a golf course.

How it works

From estimate to walkthrough

  1. 01

    Free on-site estimate

    Ron or a team member walks the property with you, measures the fence line, notes slopes, trees, utilities and gate locations, and talks through materials and styles that fit your goals and HOA.

  2. 02

    Written proposal

    You receive a written estimate with the material, height, style, footage, gates and scope spelled out, so you know exactly what is included before you sign.

  3. 03

    Planning and approvals

    We help you gather the drawings and specifications your HOA or architectural committee asks for. Utility locating is requested before any digging.

  4. 04

    Installation day

    Posts are set, framing and panels or pickets are installed and gates are hung, adjusted and latched. Many residential projects are completed in a short window once materials arrive; your proposal lists the expected schedule.

  5. 05

    Walkthrough

    We review the finished fence with you, check gate swing and latches, and make sure the site is cleaned up before we leave.

Does aluminum fencing rust?

Aluminum does not rust the way steel does, and the powder-coat finish protects the surface.

Is aluminum strong enough for dogs?

For most dogs, yes. Choose a height your dog cannot clear and add puppy pickets for small breeds.

What color aluminum is available?

Black is the standard color we install. Ask us about other finishes.
